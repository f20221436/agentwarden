# Lumora Support SLA

## First-response targets
First-response time targets by ticket priority:
- urgent: 4 hours
- high: 24 hours
- medium: 72 hours
- low: 120 hours (5 days)

## Measuring compliance
A ticket breaches the SLA when its first_response_hours value is greater than the target for its priority.
SLA compliance rate is the share of tickets whose first response met the target.

## Escalation
Urgent tickets that breach their target are escalated automatically to the on-call support lead.
