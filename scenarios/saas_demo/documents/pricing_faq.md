# Pricing FAQ

## Plans
- Lumora Free: 0 USD per month, 1 dashboard, 7-day data history.
- Lumora Basic: 9 USD per month, 10 dashboards, 90-day history.
- Lumora Pro: 29 USD per month, unlimited dashboards, 2-year history, API access.
- Lumora Enterprise: 99 USD per month, SSO (SAML), audit logs, dedicated support.

## Add-ons
Analytics Add-on 15 USD/month, Extra Storage 100GB 5 USD/month, Priority Support 25 USD/month.

## Upgrades
Upgrades take effect immediately and are pro-rated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
