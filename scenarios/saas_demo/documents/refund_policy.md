# Lumora Refund Policy

## Eligibility window
Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of a charge for any subscription plan. After 14 days,
refunds are only issued for documented service outages longer than 24 hours.

## Partial refunds
Annual plans cancelled after the eligibility window receive a pro-rated partial refund for unused full months.
Add-ons (Analytics Add-on, Extra Storage, Priority Support) are never refunded partially.

## Approval thresholds
Support agents may approve refunds up to 200 USD. Refunds above 200 USD require approval from a support
manager. Refunds above 1000 USD require approval from the finance team.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
